2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When the website is accessed, technical connection data may be processed by the hosting provider, including IP addresses, request timestamps, requested URLs, browser information and status codes. Such processing may be necessary to deliver, maintain and protect the website.

Depending on the provider's infrastructure and processing, data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Further information is available in GitHub's privacy statement.

GitHub Privacy Statement

3. Cookies and local storage

The website does not intentionally use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The language preference is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key doorbell-marketing-language so that your preferred language can be retained between visits. This setting remains in your browser until it is changed or deleted.

Local storage is distinct from HTTP cookies. Applicable legal requirements for storing information on or accessing information from your device must nevertheless be considered.

4. External links and services

The website links to external services, including GitHub, which hosts the public source code and project documentation, and Stripe, which provides an optional donation page. These services are opened through external links. When you follow a link, the respective provider processes your request under its own privacy policy. The donation page is not embedded in this website.

Stripe Privacy Policy

5. The open-source software

This website provides information about Ring HomePod Doorbell, an independently developed open-source project. The software can be installed and operated on your own hardware. The website itself does not receive doorbell events, audio recordings or smart-home configuration data through the software.

If you install or use the software, the data processed by your own installation depends on your configuration and the third-party services you choose to connect.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Where personal data is processed, the legal basis depends on the purpose and circumstances. Processing may be necessary for the legitimate interests of providing, maintaining and securing the website, subject to applicable data protection law.

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You may contact the website operator using the details in the legal notice. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.
